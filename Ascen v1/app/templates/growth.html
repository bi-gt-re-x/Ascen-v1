How it's Calculated

Each metric is scored 0–100 from your real activity and given a letter grade. Your Unified Grade Score is the average of the four metric scores.

  • Productivity — XP earned per active day.
  • Quality — average XP per completed task.
  • Consistency — share of days active since signup.
  • Efficiency — deadlines met and completion speed.

Overall = (Productivity + Quality + Consistency + Efficiency) ÷ 4

Grades and Scoring Breakdown — Details

Productivity Tracking

Measures how much XP you earn on a typical day from completed tasks and active work sessions. Your score is your average daily XP ÷ 3 (capped at 100), so averaging 300 XP per day earns a perfect 100. The more consistent daily output you put in, the higher this climbs.

Quality Tracking

Reflects how challenging the tasks you complete are, measured by their average XP value. Your score is your average XP per task × 1.75 (capped at 100), so roughly 57 XP per task earns a perfect 100. Taking on harder, higher-value work raises this over easy busywork.

Consistency Tracking

Tracks how reliably you show up over time. Your score is your active days ÷ total days since signup × 100, so being active on 18 of your first 24 days scores 75. Logging in and completing something on more days keeps this high.

Efficiency Tracking

Rewards finishing on time and quickly. Your score is 50% deadlines met + 50% speed, where speed comes from your average completion time (≤30 min scores 100, scaling down for slower tasks). Beating timers and hitting due dates both push this up.
