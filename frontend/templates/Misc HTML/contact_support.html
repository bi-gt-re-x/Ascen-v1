Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything lives locally — in your browser's local storage and in JSON files on the machine running the app. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud service. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I lost my streak — can it be restored?

Streaks count consecutive days with completed work. If a day was genuinely logged and the streak still reset, that's a bug — report it with your task history and we'll look into it.

How do XP and levels work?

Completing tasks and focus sessions earns XP. Each level requires more XP than the last, and your Growth page grades your consistency over time.

Can I use Ascen on multiple devices?

Because data is stored locally, each device keeps its own progress. To move your data, copy the app's JSON data files to the new machine.

Does Ascen cost anything?

No — Ascen is free, with every feature included. There are no paid tiers, subscriptions, or ads.

Before You Write In
